# Proposal — the-grounding-pillars-become-contract

## Why — the standard exists as instinct, not as contract

The operator's directive, after reviewing this loop's grounding
failures: predictions were being validated the way a motivated
reasoner validates a conclusion — evidence curated, simulated, or bent
toward agreement — and the discipline that catches this must become
obligations with a name, not habits. Three exhibits are on this
project's own record; a fourth happened while designing this change
and is recorded only here:

1. An analyst filed a rounding direction as settled customer intent
   with no basis in the target or the requirement (run 0006, first
   pass; the v0.9.0 record carries it).
2. The same ticket's re-entry presented a simulation of unwritten
   code in the same voice as ten verified probes — the incident
   #95's proposal records as the motivation for the analyst's
   forecast clause.
3. Two developers' self-reports each omitted their refused
   `characterize` attempt — two runs out of three; telemetry, which
   the developer cannot edit, held every attempt (the v0.10.0
   record).
4. The audit run while designing this change produced a
   conclusion-first claim of its own — a staleness finding that
   spot-checking refuted — which is the failure mode surviving inside
   the instrument built to find it.

Each incident is a different pillar failing: refutation never
attempted, provenance not demanded of a probe, recording asymmetric,
freshness judged instead of measured. The pillars name the standard;
this change makes the names contract.

## What changes

- `CLAUDE.md` and `openspec/project.md` carry the lifecycle motto —
  evidence tries to kill the claim; what survives is knowledge — and
  `project.md` names the seven grounding pillars with one-line
  obligations, as the vocabulary records, retros, and reviews cite by
  name. Pinned like the rest of the guidance.
- `agents/analyst.md` gains the obligations that follow from the
  pillars: attempt once to refute each briefing's load-bearing claim
  the target as it stands can refute — a forecast goes to its grader
  instead of being probed in advance; record every probe
  symmetrically, the inconvenient results at full fidelity; quote a
  probe's command and decisive output inside the Evidence bullet's
  claim, which the Evidence definition widens to admit — one bullet
  per file read or probe executed, in the line form the gate already
  enforces; read `approve.md`, when present, on re-entry — a ruling
  binds until the operator moves it; claim a cause only when one
  variable moved. The skill's surviving-record payload gains the
  matching `approve.md` line, so the source the contract names is one
  the driver passes.
- `openspec/specs/guidance/spec.md` and
  `openspec/specs/operation/spec.md` carry the SHALLs; the pins land
  in `test/guidance_content.bats` and `test/agents_content.bats` on
  words verified absent today (measured — the greps ran against HEAD
  before this proposal was written).

## Not built, with what would earn it

- **A lint refusing a grounding without a refutation line.** The
  obligation reuses the Evidence grammar; a dedicated line form is
  earned by the first decomposition that ships with zero refutation
  attempts and reaches a developer wrong.
- **The road-coverage script and the harness-emit fix** — the
  operator's "every path you open, you harness" as exit-code
  semantics. Next change in the program: it is a rail, so it gets its
  own red→green rather than riding a contract change.
- **Per-question approval and an `approve.md` consumption check.**
  The contract line lands here; moving `routine-approve`'s interface
  is its own change with its own tests.
- **The developer's mirror of symmetric recording.** Queued with the
  developer refinement, so this change stays one role's contract.
- **A scorecard gate.** Re-scoring the pillars per release is the
  record's habit; a script deciding scores is exactly the kind of
  abstraction the earn-abstractions law says to wait on.
